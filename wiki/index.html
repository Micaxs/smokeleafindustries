🌿 Smokeleaf Industry

A complete cannabis-cultivation and alchemy modification for Minecraft (NeoForge 1.21.1). Grow strains, process them into extracts, mix custom genetics, and build an industrial operation from humble hemp seeds.

Overview

Smokeleaf Industry transforms Minecraft agriculture into a complex production line. Start by harvesting wild hemp, progress through drying, grinding, and extraction, and eventually unlock DNA synthesis to breed custom strain genetics.

Every step preserves strain data — the THC, CBD, and effects that make each strain unique. The mod uses a generic-item + component architecture: instead of 25 separate items for each strain, a single weed item carries a StrainData component that determines its color, stats, and effects.

Quick Start

The mod follows a clear tech-tree. Here is the full progression chain:

Hemp→ Crop→ Drying→ Grinding→ Consumables→ Power→ Extraction→ Liquify→ Mixing→ Mutation→ DNA
Pro tip: You don't need to build every machine at once. Hemp → Crop → Drying Rack → Manual Grinder → Joints is a perfectly valid early-game loop. Power and electric machines come later.

Strain System

The heart of the mod. Every weed, bud, seed, and extract carries a StrainData component that defines its:

  • THC — determines how many extra effects are applied when consumed
  • CBD — scales effect duration
  • Color — derived deterministically from the strain ID

There are 25 preset strains (e.g. White Widow, OG Kush, Blue Cookies) that you can find or craft. You can also create custom strains via the Mutation and DNA systems — mix extracts in the Mixer, apply the resulting mixture in the Mutator, and get seeds with a brand-new genetic profile.

Old vs New system: The mod is mid-migration from 25 per-strain items to a generic-item + component system. Bags and gummies still use the old per-strain items; everything else (seeds, buds, weed, extract) uses the new system.

StrainData record

StrainData(int thc, int cbd)
// THC → added effect count, CBD → duration multiplier

1 · Hemp

The entry point. Find hemp plants growing in the world. When you break a plant you get:

  • hemp_leaf (×4 with shears) — craft into hemp fibers, then fabric
  • generic_bud (random strain) — your first source of usable bud

Hemp is also your source of building materials: hemp bricks, slabs, stairs, and walls.

Harvest

Hemp Leaf

Shear hemp plants for 4 leaves. Used for fibers and bio-composite.

2 · Crops & Buds

Plant generic_seeds (with StrainData) in a crop block or Grow Pot. The harvested bud preserves the strain's genetics — THC, CBD, and color carry through.

Seeds can be obtained by:

  • Breaking hemp (rare drop)
  • Crafting from buds in the Mutator
  • DNA synthesis (end-game)
Block

Grow Pot

Automated plant pot. Place seeds + fertilizer for hands-off growing.

Item

Fertilizers

Various recipes: bonemeal, bat guano, blood meal, fish emulsion, bloom booster, etc.

3 · Drying

Fresh buds contain moisture. Dry them before grinding for a bonus yield and better effects. Two options:

Machine

Drying Rack

Passive — no power required. 12 slots. Hang buds and wait.

Machine

Dryer

Electric — 2 slots, faster. Requires power.

Dried buds have the DRY = true component. The Grinder gives +1 bonus weed for dry buds.

4 · Grinding

Convert generic_bud → generic_weed. Strain data is preserved.

Tool

Manual Grinder

Hand-held. Right-click to grind. No power needed. 40 ticks per operation.

Machine

Grinder

Electric — 64,000 FE buffer. Faster throughput.

Bonus: Dry buds yield 2 weed instead of 1. Always dry before grinding!

7 · Extraction

The Extractor converts generic_weed or generic_bud into generic_extract. Strain data is preserved.

Machine

Extractor

Electric (64,000 FE). Weed → Extract or Bud → Extract. 1:1 ratio.

Extract is a key intermediate — it's used in the Liquifier and Mutator.

8 · Liquify

The Liquifier converts generic_extract into fluid extract. It also converts weed and seeds into hash oil and hemp oil.

Electric. 8,000mb tank. Extract → Fluid Extract (strain data on FluidStack).

Fluid extract carries strain data via the STRAIN_DATA component on the FluidStack — the new system for fluid-based strain tracking.

9 · Mixing

The Mixer combines two extract fluids (250 mb each) to produce 500 mb of UnidentifiedMixtureFluid with combined StrainData from both parents.

Machine

Mixer

Electric. Dual 4,000mb input tanks + 8,000mb output tank.

This is the bridge between extraction and mutation — mixed fluid carries the genetic information for new strains.

10 · Mutation

The Mutator creates new seeds with custom genetics. It requires:

  • Hemp seeds (base)
  • Base extract
  • Unidentified Mixture fluid (from the Mixer)

The output seeds carry the mixture's combined StrainData — a brand-new strain with deterministic stats derived from the strain ID.

Machine

Mutator

Electric. Seeds + Extract + Mixture → Custom-strain Seeds.

Deterministic stats: StrainUtil.finalizeMixtureStats() uses a deterministic seed from the strain ID. The same ID always gets the same THC/CBD values.

11 · DNA

The end-game. Two machines let you synthesize and sequence DNA to reproduce any of the 25 preset strains.

Machine

Synthesizer

Empty DNA Strand + 3 Reagents → Filled DNA Strand.

Machine

Sequencer

Filled DNA + Base Extract → Output with target strain. (No recipes exist yet.)

⚠️ The Sequencer currently has no recipes. There is no way to produce the 25 preset strains via DNA yet — this is a known TODO.

Tips & Known Issues

Efficiency

  • Always dry buds before grinding — you get ×2 output.
  • Set up a Generator + Grinder early for automated weed production.
  • Hemp is renewable; set up a farm for continuous leaf and seed supply.
  • Use the Drying Rack (free) for bulk drying; save the Dryer for speed.

Strain System

  • Strain data is stored in the STRAIN_DATA component. Most recipes copy it automatically.
  • Bags preserve strain when converting between bag ↔ 8 weed.
  • Custom strains from the Mutator have deterministic stats — same ID = same THC/CBD every time.
