Additional Feature Idea's:

# Edible Factory Block
A machine that has an oil input tank, a cast item input, a additional item input and an output.
This machine is used to make edibles such as gummies, and future weed infused edibles. (The cast will identify what type of edible is being made - Gummy Cast = Gummies).

# Curing Jars
A block/sealed jar where dried buds can be palced in to increase their THC potency and side effects changes/increases (CuredData component).

# Infused Drinks
Craftable Drinks (Iced Tea, soda) with straindata that gives the effects.

# Strain book
A book / catalog item that tracks discovered strains, and how to re-make the strain if you have discovered the strain before.

# Cross Breeding plants
Another way to make new strains by crossbreeding two plants A X B, where A and B are 2 plants, and X is where the new plant 25% chance its a new strain (same as mixer) otherwise its either a copy of Plant A or Plant B.

# Hemp Oil Lamp
A lamp that uses an oil to light up an area.

# Vape + Cartridges
An item that needs to be crafted alongside a filled cartridge (with a specific strain) to be used. When used, it gives the effects of the strain in the cartridge.
